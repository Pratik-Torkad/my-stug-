Who We Are

Automation hardware, sourced and built around your line

STUG Innovative Solutions is a provider of automation strategies that help manufacturing and logistics operations run more efficiently, accurately and profitably. Our engineers work alongside clients to specify the right components, then see the build through to the plant floor — from a single replacement bushing to a full custom gantry system or a servo electric actuator retrofit.

We trade in the core building blocks of linear motion and industrial automation: hard chrome plated rods, LM bearings, DU bushes, open and closed-type bearing housings, rod end bearings and supports, lead screws and ball screws with nuts, industrial extrusions and their accessories, PU flex couplings, servo electric and pneumatic actuators, and LM guideways and rails, among many other items.

Beyond stock components, we engineer to order: customised conveyor systems, material lifting equipment, material handling equipment and complete linear motion assemblies, sized and specified for the application in front of us — not adapted from someone else's.

Overview

Overview

Our induction rods are hard, ground and chrome-plated shafts used as the running surface for linear bushings, pneumatic cylinders and guide rods across general automation. Each rod is induction-hardened to a controlled case depth, then ground and chrome-plated for a low-friction, corrosion-resistant surface finish.

Available from 6mm to 150mm diameter, held to g6 tolerance, in standard lengths up to 4000mm. Larger diameters are supplied in shorter standard lengths to preserve straightness; custom lengths and end-machining are available on request.

Overview

Overview

SOB self-lubricating bushes are steel-backed bronze bushings with a pattern of solid lubricant plugs embedded in the sliding surface. As the bush wears, fresh lubricant is continuously exposed, giving stable, low-friction performance without external greasing.

They're a direct, oil-free replacement for plain bronze or oiled bushings in applications where lubrication access is difficult, or where oil contamination of the product or environment isn't acceptable. Bore is held to F7 and outer diameter to m6 for a reliable press-fit into the housing.

Overview

LM-series linear bush bearings use recirculating ball circuits running against a hardened, ground shaft to give smooth, low-friction linear travel with minimal backlash. They're the standard bearing element paired with our induction rods and chrome shafts.

The range spans 5mm to 60mm nominal shaft diameter across standard (UU), adjustable, and open-type configurations, plus a resin-retainer LUU series for lighter duty, lower-noise applications.

Overview

Overview

Housing-with-bearing units mount an LM linear bush inside a machined aluminium pillow block, giving a bolt-down bearing support that's ready to fit without separately sourcing housing and bearing. The closed LMA (SC..UU) type fully encloses the bearing; the open LMC (SME..UU) type allows a continuous support rail to pass through, for longer travel axes.

Both types ship with the linear ball bearing pre-installed and use a standard 4-bolt mounting pattern, simplifying design and replacement.

Overview

Overview

For longer travel axes, a supported shaft system carries the running rod inside a series of pedestal blocks bolted along the machine bed, instead of relying on unsupported spans. This keeps the shaft straight and rigid over long distances while remaining serviceable in sections.

LMC-C is the standard-length block; LMCL-C is a long-type block with a wider base and grease nipple for higher-load or higher-speed use. Shafts are HRC52±2 hardened, ground and chrome plated to match.

Overview

Overview

Lead screws convert rotary motion to linear feed using a simple trapezoidal thread and a matching bronze flange nut — a lower-cost, self-locking alternative to a ball screw where load and duty cycle allow. Accuracy is rated at 200 microns per 300mm of travel.

Flange nuts bolt directly to a moving carriage using a 4-hole (up to Tr22x5) or 6-hole (Tr24x5 and above) pattern, keeping the mechanical interface simple to design around.

Cross roller rails carry alternating perpendicular rollers between a V-groove rail pair, giving very high rigidity and true zero-backlash motion in a compact section — well suited to precision stages where LM bushings aren't rigid enough.

The GR series is offered in four standardised widths, each available across a range of fixed lengths, so mating dimensions stay predictable across a project.

Overview

Overview

A servo electric cylinder is a type of linear actuator that converts rotary motion from a servo motor into precise linear motion. It's widely used in automation, robotics and industrial machinery where accurate positioning, speed control and repeatability are important — replacing pneumatic or hydraulic cylinders wherever controllability matters more than raw simplicity.

At its core, a servo electric cylinder combines three elements: a servo motor for controlled rotational motion, a ball screw or lead screw mechanism that converts that rotation into linear movement, and a cylinder body and rod that deliver the final push/pull motion to the load.

Why Choose It

Key Advantages

01

Precision & Control

Servo electric actuators excel at accurate positioning, speed control and repeatability. Using feedback systems like encoders, they achieve fine motion control that pneumatic systems struggle with due to air compressibility, and that hydraulic systems struggle with due to fluid dynamics and leakage.

02

Energy Efficiency

Electric actuators only consume power when moving or holding position. Pneumatic systems require continuous compressed air, which is energy-intensive to produce, and hydraulic systems often need pumps running constantly, wasting energy.

03

Clean Operation

Electric actuators don't involve oil or compressed air, making them ideal for industries like food processing, pharmaceuticals and electronics where contamination is a concern. Hydraulic systems can leak fluid, and pneumatics can introduce moisture or particles.

04

Lower Maintenance

Fewer components — no valves, hoses, compressors or pumps — usually means less wear-and-tear and lower maintenance costs over time compared to pneumatic and hydraulic setups.

05

Easier Integration & Automation

Servo electric actuators integrate seamlessly with modern control systems (PLCs, CNC, robotics). Programming motion profiles is straightforward compared to tuning pneumatic or hydraulic systems.

06

Noise Reduction

Pneumatic systems tend to be noisy due to air exhaust and compressors. Electric actuators are much quieter in comparison.

Industrial pressing & assembly
Automotive testing & production lines
Material testing machines (tensile/compression)
Packaging and sorting systems
Medical equipment (beds, surgical tables)
Valve control in power plants
Robotics and automation systems
Aerospace testing and positioning
